# Composite Direct (FBX)

`Composite_Direct_FBX.pal` is FirebrandX's "Composite Direct (FBX)" NES palette, a direct
capture of a front-loading NTSC NES's composite output, from
<http://www.firebrandx.com/nespalette.html> (also distributed with FCEUX and Mesen).
It is his work, used here with attribution as Classic Mode's colours
(`rotj_core::palette::NES_PALETTE` holds the same 64 colours). The page states no license;
permission was requested from FirebrandX on 2026-10-16.
